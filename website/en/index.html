BUILT FOR HANDWRITTEN LEARNING

A notebook for the whole semester.

If you want a GoodNotes-like way to study on a Windows convertible, try Openote. It brings handwriting and course materials into one workspace.

✎

Think with your pen.

Use pen, ballpoint, highlighter and eraser on a free canvas. Pan and zoom with touch. Windows palm rejection prioritizes the pen. Designed for pen-enabled devices such as Surface, Galaxy Book 360, Yoga and ThinkPad models; the writing experience depends on your hardware.

▤

Work on your materials.

Import and annotate PDFs. Add images, text, equations and tables. Organize notebooks, sections and pages, plan assignments and exams, and export pages as PDF or Markdown.

▥

Your files stay with you.

Work with local notebooks without an account or a required cloud server. Create complete notebook backups and restore them later. Writing with local files works offline.

⌘

Study with an optional AI client.

Give a compatible desktop AI client read-only MCP access to a page or a selected notebook. Text and page images can include handwriting and PDF annotations. There is no built-in model; a cloud AI provider processes any content you send to it and may charge separately.

Everyday actions

Organize and export pages

Drag sections and pages into place. Hold a page over another for half a second to preview a subpage, then release. To export, right-click the page and choose “Export”, select a format and pick a destination.

Back up your notebooks

In notebook management, choose “Backup”, select some or all books and pick a destination for the ZIP file. Keep another copy on a separate drive. Use “Import” to restore a backup.

Connect an AI study client

Open the AI icon, enable access, choose the current page or a notebook, then paste the connection instructions from “Copy for chat” into a desktop AI client that supports local MCP connections. Keep Openote open. Access is read-only, excludes locked pages and resets to off after restarting. Handwriting is provided as images, not searchable OCR. The connection text includes a secret token.

BEFORE YOU SWITCH

Is Openote
right for you?

Can I import OneNote notebooks?

A limited importer supports local .one and .onepkg files through the optional native Rust core, which the release workflow builds. Choose “Import” in notebook management. Not all content or layouts are supported: check imported pages and keep the originals. Full migration and faithful editing are not guaranteed. There is no direct OneDrive integration.

What about Linux, macOS and Android editing?

Published packages target Windows x64 and the Android companion scanner. Android is for scanning, not full notebook editing. Linux source remains in the repository, but Linux and macOS downloads are not currently offered.

How do updates work?

The Windows app and Android scanner check GitHub for newer releases. You confirm installation. Android updates must use the same signing key. You can also download the latest version here.

Is it free?

Yes. App and scanner are open source under AGPL-3.0-or-later; some components have their own licenses. Optional external AI services may charge fees. Openote is independent and is not affiliated with Microsoft or GoodNotes.
